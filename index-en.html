Mala Duba

Mala Duba is one of the rare places in Dalmatia that has preserved its original beauty.
This hidden gem offers you sun, crystal clear sea, relaxation in the shade of pine trees
and a beautiful kilometer-long seaside promenade.

Beautiful pebble beaches stretch north and south of the village, offering countless spots for carefree sunbathing. Along the kilometer-long seaside promenade, in the shade of pine trees, you can enjoy peaceful walks or cycling and discover the surrounding villages and nature.
